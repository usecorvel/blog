# About the blog

Corvel answers appointment texts for more than 200 clinics and salons. That puts us in an odd
spot: we see an enormous number of the small conversations that decide whether a chair or a
treatment room is full on a Tuesday afternoon. This blog is where we write down what those
conversations teach us.

## What we write about

- **Cancellations and no-shows.** What they cost, when they happen and what actually reduces them.
- **Waitlists.** How to run one that fills slots instead of annoying people.
- **Reminders and messaging.** Wording, timing and what clients reply to.
- **The front desk.** The work that keeps a clinic or salon running and rarely gets written about.

When we quote numbers, they come from Corvel's own aggregate data across our customers, with
nothing that identifies a business or a client. We say so when a figure comes from a smaller
sample.

## Who writes it

Mostly Priya Raman (co-founder, previously ran scheduling for a physical therapy group), Hana
Sato (customer success, previously managed a salon) and Marcus Bell (engineering, who built the
waitlist engine).
